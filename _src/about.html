MeowWise is a free, independent guide to cat care, health, behavior, nutrition and breeds, written in plain language for cat owners and people thinking about getting a cat. Ownership and legal details are on our terms page.

How we write

  • Answer first. Every page starts with a direct answer to its main question, then explains the detail.
  • Sourced. We base our guides on veterinary schools (such as the Cornell Feline Health Center and the UC Davis School of Veterinary Medicine), veterinary associations (AAFP, AAHA, AVMA, WSAVA), cat welfare charities (International Cat Care, Cats Protection, RSPCA), government agencies and peer reviewed research. Each guide lists its sources at the bottom.
  • No made up facts. If we cannot find a reliable source for a claim, we leave it out.
  • Original writing. All text and illustrations on MeowWise are original.
  • Updated. Each guide shows the date it was last updated. When guidance changes, we revise the page.

What MeowWise is not

MeowWise is general education. We are not veterinarians and nothing here replaces an examination by a vet who knows your cat. If you are worried about your cat's health, contact your veterinarian; in an emergency, go to the nearest emergency vet.

Advertising and independence

MeowWise may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Those ads are not our recommendations, and nobody pays us to change a guide. How we write, how AI generated images are labelled, and how ads stay separate from editorial content is on our editorial and AI disclosure. Cookie and opt-out details are in our privacy policy and disclaimer.

Corrections and suggestions

Spotted an error, or have a question you would like us to answer? Send us a message through our contact form. We read every message and correct mistakes promptly.
