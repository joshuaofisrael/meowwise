This page explains where the photo-style images on MeowWise come from, how each kind is labelled, and what the Source link under a photo leads to. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Two kinds of images

MeowWise uses two kinds of photo-style images: AI generated photorealistic illustrations, and real photographs of real cats. Every photo's caption says which kind it is, and the Source link under that caption leads to this page.

AI generated images

Photos captioned "AI generated" were created for MeowWise with an AI image tool. They are not photographs of a real event, a real person, or a real, identifiable cat; they are illustrative images made to sit beside a guide or blog post and show what the text is describing. They are original to MeowWise and are not taken from any other site. Each one is logged with its origin and license in the site's image records.

Real cat photographs

Photos without an "AI generated" label are real photographs of real cats. Every one of them is a CC0 1.0 public domain dedication by its photographer on Wikimedia Commons. We checked the license on each photo's own Commons page before using it, and we credit the photographer even though CC0 does not legally require it.

Where the full list lives

Every photo on MeowWise, AI generated or real, is listed with its details on our photo credits page: the source file, photographer, and license for each real photo, and a short description for each AI generated image. Drawings, icons, and game art on this site are original works by MeowWise and are not photographs. If you have a question about a specific image, use the contact page.
