MeowWise is a brand of Joshua Israel Ventures LLC. This page explains who writes our guides, how AI generated images are labelled, and how advertising is kept separate from that writing. Last updated 5 November 2026.

How we write

Guides, blog posts and classroom pages are original writing by the MeowWise team. Each guide answers its main question first, then explains the detail, and lists the veterinary schools, associations, charities, government agencies or papers it relies on. If we cannot find a reliable source for a claim, we leave it out. Nothing here is veterinary advice for an individual cat. The longer version of this process is on our about page.

AI generated images

Some photorealistic pictures on MeowWise were generated with an AI image tool for this site. They illustrate the topic of the page. They are not photographs of a real event, a real person or a specific cat. Each one is labelled in its caption as AI generated, and the full set is listed on our photo credits page. Drawings, icons and game art are original works by MeowWise. Photographs of real cats are public domain images and are credited on that same page.

Advertising

MeowWise may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Google chooses which ads appear. An ad is not a recommendation from us, and it does not change what a guide says. We do not sell article placement, paid links or affiliate links. Cookie use and how to opt out of personalized ads are in our privacy policy.

Corrections

If a page is wrong, or an image is missing its AI label, tell us through the contact form. We read every message.
